# Demonstration video: shot by shot

**Team USV · Virasat · Global Innovation Hackathon 2026**

Target length: 3 minutes. Record against the live deployment at
https://virasat-indol.vercel.app so the judges see the same thing they can open.

Before you start: open the site, press **Reset demo** on /try, set the theme to
Light and the language to English. Close other tabs so notifications stay out of
the recording. Record at 1920x1080.

---

## 0:00 to 0:20 — The problem, on the landing page

**On screen:** the home page hero, then scroll slowly to the four statistics.

**Say:** "About one lakh eighty four thousand crore rupees of Indians' own money
is sitting unclaimed in banks, insurance, provident fund and shares. It is not
lost. It is just that families do not know it exists, and the way to claim it is
different at every institution. This is Virasat."

---

## 0:20 to 0:50 — Find: a photo becomes an asset map

**On screen:** /try, Find tab. Sunita's three papers are already loaded. Press
**Use these papers**. Let the asset map appear. Point at the confidence badges.

**Say:** "Sunita has a box of her father's old papers. She photographs them. We
read each one and pull out the institution, the account number and the amount,
with a confidence score on every single field, so nothing is silently guessed.
Then we add his income tax statement, the AIS, which lists every bank and company
that ever paid him interest or a dividend. That one document reveals accounts the
family never knew about. No other product does this."

---

## 0:50 to 1:35 — Claim: the rule engine and the Two AI Debate

**On screen:** Claim tab. Pick the SBI row. Answer the three questions. Press
**Get my route**. When the verdict appears, expand the full debate.

**Say:** "Now the important part. The route to the money is decided by a rule
engine, not by the AI, because the rule engine is versioned and tested and gives
the same answer every time. Here it says: no nominee, under five lakh rupees, so
the simplified legal heir route applies, no court needed. Then two AI reviewers
argue about it. One supports the route, one attacks it, and a referee gives the
verdict. The challenger caught a real problem here. And notice the referee cannot
override the rule engine: that is enforced in code, not in the prompt."

---

## 1:35 to 2:05 — The claim pack, tracking and prevention

**On screen:** Download the claim pack PDF and open it. Then the Track tab, then
the Vault tab.

**Say:** "Virasat fills the forms and produces a claim pack: the claim form, a
cover letter in Hindi and English, and the exact checklist of what to attach.
Every claim is tracked, with an escalation to the ombudsman after thirty days.
And the Vault stops this from happening again, by checking that every account in
the family has a nominee."

---

## 2:05 to 2:35 — Built for Bharat: Hindi, voice, dark mode, accessibility

**On screen:** Switch the language to Hindi. Show the route and the checklist in
Hindi. Press the speaker to hear it read aloud. Switch to dark mode. Press an
info button. Resize the window narrow to show it works on a phone.

**Say:** "Every screen is in Hindi, including the rule engine output and the
debate itself, not just the menus. It reads aloud, for anyone who cannot read
comfortably. Light and dark, Simple and Technical modes, and an info button on
every term. Zero serious accessibility violations across ten pages, measured with
axe, and Lighthouse ninety six, one hundred, one hundred, one hundred on the live
site."

---

## 2:35 to 3:00 — Scale, and the close

**On screen:** /developers, showing the API playground. Then /judges briefly.

**Say:** "For scale, all of this is a public API. An institution can bring their
own data, their own AI key, and their own claim rules, and every answer records
which rule version decided it, so a compliance team can audit it. Free for
families. Institutions and service centres pay. Aapki poonji, aapka adhikar.
Your money, your right. Thank you."
